# Prompt 11 — Nutrition and recovery

Implement the P1 nutrition and sleep surfaces shown in the supplied `Benchless screens/` references.

## Requirements

- Calorie target: provide transparent editable estimate inputs; avoid presenting targets as medical advice or certainty.
- Food: daily total, meals, search/add food, portion and nutrition details, edit/remove, and empty/loading/error states. Use a licensed/approved food catalogue or clearly labeled local sample catalogue.
- Sleep: capture bedtime, wake time, calculated duration, and a simple user-rated sleep quality; permit correction and show trends only from logged entries.
- Keep these optional and separate from injury safety decisions. Avoid shame, coercive language, or unsupported health claims.

## Acceptance

- Users can create, edit, and remove their own logs; values are derived from saved state.
- No seeded example becomes indistinguishable from a user's own health data.
- Forms remain accessible and work with numeric/time keyboards.

Implement only nutrition and sleep, report data/copy limitations and stop for review.
